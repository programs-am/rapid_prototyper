# Founder OS — Prototyper

Turn customer insights into prototypes you can use to get customer feedback. Your AI agent coaches you
step by step to make every decision.

> Adapted from the Founder OS workspace by WNCP AI (see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)).

## Get started

You need an AI coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Google Antigravity, Cursor, Gemini CLI or similar),
plus [git](https://git-scm.com/downloads) and [Node](https://nodejs.org/) installed. Open your agent
and paste:

```text
Clone https://github.com/programs-am/rapid_prototyper.git into a new folder and work inside it.
Read AGENTS.md, then get me started: run the setup, and interview me one question at a time
to build my founder profile and company context. If you can't run the setup script, make the
same links by hand. Tell me if I need to restart the session. Keep explanations short and plain.
```

Already cloned it? Open the folder in your agent and say **"get me started."**

Bring real notes from customer research or conversations if you have them. If you don't have any, you
can still start: your outputs will be marked as light on evidence, and the agent can help you prepare
for customer interviews.

For the full walkthrough (folders, stages, prototype types and a worked example), read
[FOUNDER-GUIDE.md](FOUNDER-GUIDE.md).

## Good to know

- **Your raw data stays on your machine.** `5_Library/sources/raw/` is never uploaded to GitHub.
- **No model key needed.** Bring your own agent.
- **Nothing is invented.** Unproven quotes, numbers or claims are marked `[PLACEHOLDER]`.
- **Prototypes are tests, not products.** They will not take real payments. Legal or safety questions should go to a professional.
- **Plain templates** for every step can be found in `5_Library/templates/`.

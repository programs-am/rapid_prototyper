# C.2-Metrics-OKR

**Purpose:** write one Objective and two to four Key Results that act as validation signals for the riskiest assumption in your PRD. These are not activity counts. Apply two checks: a self-check ("did the bet pay off, or were you just busy?") and an ambition check (aim for a 50-60% hit rate, so targets stretch without being fantasy). The output block drops straight into PRD v2 section 8.

**Typical time:** 45-60 minutes.

## Inputs

- PRD v1, especially the riskiest assumption in section 7
- The ranked jobs from A.4
- The note you chose to act on from peer review (C.1), if you did one

## What you do by hand

- Open `C.2-Metrics-OKR-Template.md` (or the `.docx` version).
- Write the riskiest assumption in one sentence.
- Write the Objective: one qualitative, time-bound direction, not a metric.
- Write 2-4 Key Results, each a measurable signal that proves or disproves the assumption, and tag each with a signal type (acquisition, activation, retention, revenue, referral, or whatever fits your product). Examples across industries are in a comment at the bottom of the template: pre-orders, trial orders from retail buyers, repeat purchases, a trial part running a full shift.
- Run both checks on every Key Result and rewrite the ones that fail.

## What the skill writes

`metrics` surfaces your top riskiest assumptions from the PRD and JTBD and coaches the Objective and Key Results, either from scratch or by stress-testing your draft. It writes `2_Outputs/.agents/C.2-Metrics-OKR/Metrics-OKR.md`. It does not fill in your template.

## Done when

- Every Key Result is something you could check next week with a number or a yes/no.
- If every Key Result came in at 100%, you would be surprised. If every one came in at 0%, you would know what to change.

Paste your Objective and Key Results into PRD v2 section 8 by hand. Your completed template stays in this folder. The blank master is mirrored in `5_Library/templates/`.

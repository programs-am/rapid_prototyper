# C.1-Peer-Review

**Purpose:** put your riskiest assumption and your prototype in front of peers or advisors and get one high-signal note back. You are not presenting the whole product. You are asking: where is the weakest point, and what would break it?

**Typical time:** about 6 minutes per founder in a group; 60-90 minutes for a group of 8-12. Solo, send your write-up to two or three peers and allow a day or two for replies.

## Inputs

- Your PRD v1 (especially the riskiest assumption in section 7) and your prototype
- Notes and next actions from the advisor review (B), if you did one

## What you do by hand

- Prepare one slide, page, or photo that states the single assumption you most need to be wrong about, and what your prototype shows about it.
- Choose a format:
  - **Strict-timer round (recommended for groups).** For example 2 minutes to present, 2 minutes of questions, 2 minutes for each reviewer to hand you one written note. A visible timer keeps it honest.
  - **Async.** Send the page and the prototype to a few people with the question "what is the weakest point?" and ask for a single note back.
- As a reviewer, give exactly one highest-signal note per founder: the thing you would test next if it were your product.
- Read every note, then pick the one you will act on and write it down. Ignore the rest for now.

There is no template for this stage. Keep the notes in a short file in this folder.

## What the skill writes

No pipeline skill runs at this stage. The note you act on feeds C.2 (metrics) and C.3 (PRD v2). Any derived notes go to `2_Outputs/.agents/C.1-Peer-Review/`.

## Done when

- You have one clear note you trust, and you can say how it changes your riskiest assumption or your prototype.

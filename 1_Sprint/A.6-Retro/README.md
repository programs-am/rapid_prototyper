# A.6-Retro

**Purpose:** a short retro after your first build. Name one thing that broke and the one thing it taught you. Fast prototyping creates breakage by design, and saying it out loud makes the mess useful instead of embarrassing.

**Typical time:** 10-20 minutes solo. In a group, about 2 minutes per person plus a few minutes of discussion.

## Inputs

- Your first prototype (A.5), however rough
- Your PRD v1 and whatever you learned while building

## What you do by hand

- Write two lines: **What broke** (a prototype that did not work, a sample that failed, an assumption customers laughed at, a supplier that fell through) and **What it taught me** (what you will do differently, or what you now believe that you did not before).
- Be specific. "The label peeled off in the cold chain test, so I now test packaging before flavors" is useful. "Packaging is hard" is not.
- In a group, share both lines aloud. Pick any format you like, including a lighthearted award for the best breakage.
- Carry the lesson into the next stage: it often changes what B or C.1 should focus on, and it belongs in PRD v2.

There is no template for this stage. Keep your two lines in a short note in this folder.

## What the skill writes

No pipeline skill runs at this stage. If you ask your agent to help turn the lesson into a PRD v2 change, any derived notes go to `2_Outputs/.agents/A.6-Retro/`.

## Done when

- You have one broken thing and one lesson written down, and you know where the lesson will show up next.

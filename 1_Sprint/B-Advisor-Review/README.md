# B-Advisor-Review

**Purpose:** a 1:1 working review of your PRD and prototype with someone who knows more than you about some part of your problem: a mentor, an advisor, or a domain expert (a retail buyer, a manufacturer, a lawyer, a clinician, a senior engineer, a marketer). You own the agenda. You leave with concrete next actions.

**Typical time:** about 30 minutes per review (roughly 25 of working time plus a short wrap-up). Allow 30-60 minutes beforehand to prepare.

## Inputs

- PRD v1 and your prototype (A.5)
- Your retro lines (A.6), if you wrote them
- A short list of the questions you most want answered

## What you do by hand

- Choose the reviewer for the weakest part of your plan. If the biggest risk is manufacturing, find someone who has manufactured; if it is distribution, find someone who has sold into that channel.
- Send the PRD and a link or photo of the prototype ahead of time, with three questions you want answered.
- Run the conversation yourself. Cover defensibility (why you, why this, why now), your riskiest assumption, and scope (what to cut).
- Close by writing down next actions: who does what, by when. Note anything the reviewer would change before you show peers.

There is no template for this stage. Keep your notes and next actions in a short file in this folder.

## What the skill writes

No pipeline skill runs at this stage. If you want help turning the notes into PRD v2 edits, ask your agent and any derived notes go to `2_Outputs/.agents/B-Advisor-Review/`.

## Done when

- You have a list of next actions with owners, and you know your riskiest assumption better than you did before the review.
- You know what to bring to C.1 (peer review) and which parts of PRD v2 need to change.

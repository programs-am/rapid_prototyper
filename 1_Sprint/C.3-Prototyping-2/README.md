# C.3-Prototyping-2

**Stage:** refine or rebuild your prototype from what you learned. About 2–3 hours of work, plus the
time to run the next test.

**Skill:** `prototype-kit` in round-2 mode — say "refine my prototype". It reads `prototype-v1.md`, your
test results and feedback, and your PRD, then helps you pick the single highest-signal change.

**Inputs:** `4_Prototypes/<slug>/prototype-v1.md`, feedback from peers/advisors/testers (stage C.1,
B), your PRD v2 if you have one.

**You do by hand:** write down the feedback in your own words, choose the one change, and run the next
test. Follow the [Refine Guide](Refine-Guide.md).

**Where things land:** `4_Prototypes/<slug>/prototype-v2.md` (v1 is kept) and updated spec docs.

# A.5-Prototyping-1

**Stage:** turn your PRD into something a real person can react to. About 2–4 hours for a first
version, depending on your product (a clickable flow or a landing page takes hours; a physical sample
or a hand-run service pilot takes longer and is mostly waiting and testing).

**Skill:** `prototype-kit` — say "set up my prototype". It picks your prototype lane (software, website,
physical product, CPG, fashion, marketing/comms, or service), decides how far to take it, writes the
spec documents, can build the first version with you, and writes the test plan.

**Inputs:** your PRD (`2_Outputs/.agents/A.5-PRD-v1/PRD-v1.md`), your product type, and the lane guides in
`5_Library/prototyping/lanes/`.

**You do by hand:** decide the one thing the prototype must prove, read each spec document and correct
it, and run the test with real people. The spec templates in `templates/` are yours to edit.

**Where things land:** your kit in `4_Prototypes/<slug>/` (PRD, spec docs, the prototype, `prototype-v1.md`);
the agent's index in `2_Outputs/.agents/A.5-Prototyping-1/`.

Read the [Prototyping Guide](Prototyping-Guide.md) first if you want the whole picture.

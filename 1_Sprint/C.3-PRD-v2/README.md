# C.3-PRD-v2

**Stage:** extend PRD v1 into the full nine-section v2 — adding market dynamics, assumptions and risks,
OKRs (from the metrics stage) and a roadmap. About 1–2 hours.

**Skill:** `prd` in v2 mode — say "extend my PRD".

**Inputs:** your PRD v1, metrics/OKRs (stage C.2), test results and feedback from prototype round 1.

**You do by hand:** fill in `C.3-PRD-v2-Template.md`. Triage feedback to the single highest-signal change
and refine that change, not the whole product, so the next round stays testable. Your riskiest
assumption in Assumptions & Risks is what your OKRs must prove.

**Where things land:** your template stays here; the agent's derived `PRD-v2.md` goes to
`2_Outputs/.agents/C.3-PRD-v2/` (v1 is kept unchanged). A Word version is not shipped; ask your agent to
export the Markdown to .docx if you prefer Word.

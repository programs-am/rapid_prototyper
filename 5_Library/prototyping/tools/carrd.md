# Carrd

Simple builder for one-page sites; the fastest way to put up a landing page with a signup or contact form.

- **Pricing:** Has a free tier at time of writing; check current pricing.
- **Pricing note:** Prices and limits change often; last reviewed Jun 2026 — check the vendor's site before relying on it.
- **Best for:** A founder who just needs a single landing page to test interest. Pick Carrd for speed and simplicity.

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Go to carrd.co and sign up
2. Start from a template (landing page or profile) or blank
3. Edit the headline, description, images and button; keep the page to one clear call to action
4. Add a form element or link out to a Tally form for waitlist signups
5. Publish to a Carrd subdomain; custom domains and some form features need a paid plan

**Watch First:** Search YouTube for "Carrd landing page tutorial"

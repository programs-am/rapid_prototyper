# Tally

Form builder for waitlists, surveys, pre-order interest and feedback; it works like typing in a document.

- **Pricing:** Has a free tier at time of writing; check current pricing.
- **Pricing note:** Prices and limits change often; last reviewed Jun 2026 — check the vendor's site before relying on it.
- **Best for:** A founder who wants to collect emails, survey answers or pre-order interest to validate demand before building.
- **Privacy:** Do not collect sensitive data (health, financial, government ID, children's details). Ask only what you need.

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Go to tally.so and sign up
2. Create a new form and add questions by typing; keep it to a few short questions (email, what they want, how much they would pay or how soon)
3. Add a clear consent line, e.g. 'I agree to be contacted about this product', and a short note on how you will use their answers
4. Preview it, then publish and copy the link; embed it in a Carrd, Framer or Webflow page or share it directly
5. Review responses, and export or connect them to a spreadsheet to track results

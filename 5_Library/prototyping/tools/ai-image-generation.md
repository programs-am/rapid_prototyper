# AI Image Generation

Generate concept visuals — product renders, packaging looks, apparel flats and lifestyle shots — from text prompts using image models built into major AI assistants or dedicated tools.

- **Pricing:** Has a free tier at time of writing; check current pricing. Examples: the image features in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude-adjacent assistants, plus Midjourney, Ideogram and Adobe Firefly.
- **Pricing note:** Prices and limits change often; last reviewed Jun 2026 — check the vendor's site before relying on it.
- **Best for:** A founder who needs a convincing look-and-feel picture of a product, label or collection to test reactions before anything is made.
- **Warning:** Models still garble small text on labels, ingredient panels and logos. Overlay real text in a design tool.
- **Rights:** Check each tool's commercial-use terms, and avoid prompting with other brands or real people. Treat outputs as concept art, not manufacturing files.

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Pick a tool: an assistant you already use for convenience, Ideogram or Firefly for text-heavy or brand-safe work, Midjourney for stylized looks
2. Write a prompt with subject, material, shape, setting, lighting and camera angle — e.g. 'matte black stainless water bottle, studio product shot, soft shadow, white background, 3/4 view'
3. Use a pattern per need: product render (material + background + angle), packaging (box or pouch type + colour palette + shelf setting), apparel flat (front view, flat lay, plain background, garment details), lifestyle (person, place, time of day, mood)
4. Generate several variations, pick the best, and re-prompt with one change at a time (colour, angle, background)
5. Export, then add real text, logos and barcodes yourself in Canva, Figma or similar — do not trust the model's lettering

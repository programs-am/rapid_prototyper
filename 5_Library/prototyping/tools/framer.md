# Framer

Visual website builder with AI page generation and polished animations; good for a modern marketing site or product page.

- **Pricing:** Has a free tier at time of writing; check current pricing.
- **Pricing note:** Prices and limits change often; last reviewed Jun 2026 — check the vendor's site before relying on it.
- **Best for:** A founder who wants a good-looking, design-forward site with smooth animation and minimal setup. Pick Framer when look and feel matter most.

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Go to framer.com and sign up
2. Start a new project and either use the AI page generator with a short brief or pick a template close to your idea
3. Replace placeholder text and images with your real copy, product shots and logo
4. Check the mobile and tablet views in the breakpoint switcher and fix layout issues
5. Publish to a free Framer subdomain to share a link; connect a custom domain later (usually a paid step)

# Onshape

Cloud-based professional CAD tool — build detailed 3D models and technical drawings directly in the browser. Free for public projects.

- **Pricing:** Free for public documents. Professional plans from $1,500/year (not needed for prototyping).
- **Pricing note:** Prices and limits change often; last reviewed Jun 2026 — check the vendor's site before relying on it.
- **Best for:** A founder who needs dimensioned 3D parts or technical drawings to hand to a manufacturer or print service.

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Go to onshape.com and sign up for a free account with Google or email
2. Click 'Create' → 'Document' to start a new project
3. Use the Sketch tool to draw a 2D cross-section of your part, then use 'Extrude' to turn it into a 3D shape
4. Add features (holes, fillets, chamfers) from the toolbar to refine the model
5. Click 'Export' to download as STL, STEP, or PDF — or share the document link directly for review

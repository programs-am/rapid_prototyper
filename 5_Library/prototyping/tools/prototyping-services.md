# Prototyping Services

Category guide to outside services that can make your first physical samples: 3D-print and CNC shops, PCB fabricators, food labs, pattern makers and sample rooms.

- **Pricing:** Pricing varies by job and vendor; get quotes. No prices are listed here.
- **Pricing note:** Prices and limits change often; last reviewed Jun 2026 — check the vendor's site before relying on it.
- **Best for:** A founder who has a design, recipe or pattern and needs someone else to build the first sample.
- **Questions to ask:** What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? What is the lead time for a sample and for a repeat order? What files or formats do you need? Who owns the design and recipe? What is the revision and rework policy? For food: what licences, labelling and allergen controls apply?

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Pick the right type of service. Physical products: online 3D-print and CNC service bureaus, PCB fabrication and assembly services, local makerspaces or fab labs. CPG: test kitchens, small-batch co-packers, university food-science labs. Fashion: pattern makers, sample rooms, local sewing studios, print-on-demand
2. Prepare a short brief: what it is, quantity, target price, materials or ingredients, and photos or sketches
3. Attach files: STL or STEP for parts, Gerber and bill of materials for circuit boards, formula and spec sheet for food, tech pack (sketches, measurements, fabric and trims) for garments
4. Ask for quotes from two or three providers and compare
5. Order one sample first, test it, write down what to change, and only then discuss larger runs

# Gamma

AI tool that generates decks, documents and simple web pages from a prompt or outline.

- **Pricing:** Has a free tier at time of writing; check current pricing.
- **Pricing note:** Prices and limits change often; last reviewed Jun 2026 — check the vendor's site before relying on it.
- **Best for:** A founder who needs a pitch, customer or investor deck fast and will refine the story afterward.
- **Note:** AI-written slides can include invented claims or numbers. Check everything before sharing.

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Go to gamma.app and sign up
2. Choose to create a presentation and paste an outline or a short prompt with audience, problem, solution and ask
3. Pick a theme, generate, and review every slide for accuracy
4. Edit the text and swap in your real images, product renders and data; delete anything you cannot support
5. Present from the browser, share a link, or export to PDF or PowerPoint

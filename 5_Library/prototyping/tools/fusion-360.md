# Fusion (Fusion 360)

Autodesk's CAD and CAM software for designing parts, assemblies and enclosures that can be 3D printed or manufactured.

- **Pricing:** Offers a free personal-use tier with eligibility conditions (for example non-commercial use); check Autodesk's current terms to see whether a startup qualifies, and expect to need a paid plan for commercial work.
- **Pricing note:** Prices and limits change often; last reviewed Jun 2026 — check the vendor's site before relying on it.
- **Best for:** A founder who needs precise, editable 3D parts (enclosures, brackets, moulds) and is ready to learn real CAD.
- **Note:** Work in millimetres, model one part at a time, and check wall thickness against your chosen process.

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Check Autodesk's eligibility page to confirm which licence applies to you, then sign in or create an Autodesk account
2. Download and install Fusion on a Mac or Windows computer
3. Start a new design and create a sketch of your part's outline with dimensions
4. Use Extrude, Fillet and Hole to build the part; keep a timeline of features so you can edit values later
5. Export as STL for 3D printing or STEP for a manufacturer or service bureau

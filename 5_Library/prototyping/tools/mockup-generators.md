# Mockup Generators

Category guide to tools that place your artwork onto realistic devices, packaging and apparel so you can show how a product will look.

- **Pricing:** Has a free tier at time of writing; check current pricing. Many mockup sites have free samples with paid downloads.
- **Pricing note:** Prices and limits change often; last reviewed Jun 2026 — check the vendor's site before relying on it.
- **Best for:** A founder who already has artwork (logo, label, UI screens) and needs a believable picture of it on a phone, box, bottle or t-shirt.
- **Note:** A mockup shows intent, not a manufacturable product. Dielines from generators should be checked by your packaging supplier. Check each site's licence for commercial use of exported images.

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Choose by need: Placeit-style sites for device, packaging and apparel scenes; Pacdora-style tools for custom packaging dielines and 3D box mockups; Printful or Printify for apparel and merch
2. Prepare your artwork as PNG or SVG at the correct aspect ratio (use a transparent background for logos)
3. Upload it to the template, then adjust colours, angles and scene; for packaging, enter your box dimensions to generate a dieline and fold it into 3D
4. For apparel, connect a print-on-demand account, choose the garment and place the design to see vendor mockups; order one sample to check fabric and print quality
5. Export images for a deck, landing page or pre-order test; keep the dieline file to send to a printer or packaging supplier later

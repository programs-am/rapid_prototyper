# Canva

Browser-based design tool for packaging and label artwork, social posts, decks and simple mockups, with a brand kit to keep colours and fonts consistent.

- **Pricing:** Has a free tier at time of writing; check current pricing.
- **Pricing note:** Prices and limits change often; last reviewed Jun 2026 — check the vendor's site before relying on it.
- **Best for:** A non-designer who needs presentable labels, packaging artwork, social graphics or a pitch deck quickly.
- **Note:** Canva is good for concept and early artwork. Many printers want production-ready files with exact dielines; confirm requirements before ordering.

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Go to canva.com and sign up with email or Google
2. Search the template gallery for your format (label, flyer, Instagram post, presentation) or use a custom size matching your real label or box dimensions
3. Set up your brand: add logo, colours and fonts (Brand Kit may be a paid feature; check your plan) and apply them across designs
4. Add your own text, product images and any AI-generated art; use the Apps panel to search for mockup options that place your design on a bottle, box, bag or garment
5. Share a link for feedback, or download PDF or PNG; for print, check the printer's required file type, bleed and colour mode
